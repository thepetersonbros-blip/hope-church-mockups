Hope Youth.

Students and young adults growing in God’s Word and in friendship with one another.

Youth Group

Growing up in the faith.

Our students have their own Sunday School class at 9 AM during the school year. Youth group also gets together for fellowship, Bible study, and fun throughout the year.

Check Coming Up at Hope on our home page for the next youth group gathering.

Young Adults

Friendship around the Word.

The young adults of Hope gather to study the Bible, pray, and do life together. Whether you are in college, starting a career, or somewhere in between, we would love to have you.
